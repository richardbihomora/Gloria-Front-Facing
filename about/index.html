Our Story

Family engagement shouldn't feel like a second job for your team.

We built Gloria on a simple premise: keeping families connected to daily life in senior living communities should be effortless for everyone involved. Having experienced firsthand how difficult it can be to stay connected with our own elderly loved ones, we created Gloria to bridge that gap.

We knew any real solution had to be two things: deeply family focused and incredibly easy for staff to use. That's why Gloria combines an intuitive experience with AI-assisted drafts, making it possible to create meaningful family updates in seconds, not minutes. From photos and everyday moments to announcements, newsletters, and events, Gloria gives communities one simple place to keep families informed, engaged, and connected.

The result is stronger family relationships, greater confidence in your community, and more time for staff to focus on what matters most: caring for residents.
