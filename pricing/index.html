Pricing questions

Everything you need to know about Gloria’s pricing. Have a question we haven’t answered? Reach out to us at dave@gloriatech.co.

How is Gloria charged?

Gloria is billed monthly based on the highest number of active residents during that month.

What if we move to a different pricing tier?

Your monthly rate automatically adjusts based on the number of active residents in Gloria.

Is there a setup or cancellation fee?

No. Onboarding and account setup are included, and you can cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

Is there a limit to the number of staff or family members?

Family members are unlimited. The number of staff accounts cannot exceed the number of residents active in Gloria.

Is there a difference between the pricing tiers?

No. All pricing tiers include the same features. We may introduce optional features in the future.

Is there a minimum resident requirement?

Yes. A minimum of 25 residents must be enrolled in Gloria.
