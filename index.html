What is Gloria?

Gloria is a family communication platform built specifically for senior living communities. Staff share photos, updates, announcements, newsletters, and events with families in one simple, private app.

Who is Gloria for?

Senior living communities — independent living, assisted living, and memory care — and the families of their residents.

How does Gloria work?

Staff post from a simple staff app, approved family members follow along in the family app, and admins manage accounts, permissions, and content from the admin portal.

How is Gloria charged?

Monthly, based on the highest number of residents active in Gloria that month.

What if we move to a different pricing tier?

Your rate is automatically adjusted based on the number of residents active in Gloria.

Is there a setup or cancellation fee?

No. Onboarding and account setup are included, and you can cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

Is there a limit to the number of staff or family members?

No — family members are unlimited. You cannot have more staff members than residents active in Gloria.

Is there a difference between the pricing tiers?

No. All pricing tiers include the same features, although we may add optional features in the future.

Is there a minimum resident requirement?

Yes. We require a minimum of 25 residents to be enrolled in Gloria.
