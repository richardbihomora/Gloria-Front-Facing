What is Gloria?

Gloria is a communication platform that connects care staff with families, streamlining updates and ensuring transparency in long-term care.

Who is Gloria for?

Gloria is built for care facilities and the families of their residents, making communication easier and transparent.

How does the Gloria app work?

Gloria lets care staff share photos, wellness updates, announcements, a monthly newsletter, and a shared calendar with families — keeping everyone connected, informed, and coordinated. Direct family messaging is coming in v2.
