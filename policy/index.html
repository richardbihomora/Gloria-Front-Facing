Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Gloria ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and the Gloria mobile application (together, the "Services"). Gloria is a communication platform that helps residential care facilities keep families connected with their loved ones through photos, wellness updates, announcements, newsletters, calendars, and supply requests. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected.

Gloria may update this policy from time to time by amending this page. Please check this page periodically to ensure you are happy with any changes. This policy is effective as of 01.25.25 and last updated 08.08.26.

Who Uses Gloria

The Gloria app is used by two kinds of people: staff members at care facilities that subscribe to Gloria, and family members of residents at those facilities. Accounts are created by or at the direction of the care facility — Gloria does not offer self-service public sign-up. The care facility decides which residents each account may see, and remains responsible for the accuracy of the information it shares through Gloria.

Information We Collect

Account information. When a care facility creates an account for you, we collect your name, email address, role (staff or family), phone number, and an optional profile photo.

Resident content. Care facility staff share content about residents through Gloria: photos, videos, wellness updates, announcements, and calendar events. This content may include health-related information about a resident. It is provided by the care facility, which is responsible for obtaining any consents required to share it, and it is visible only to staff at the facility and to family members the facility has linked to that resident.

Relationship information. Family accounts are linked to one or more residents with a relationship label (for example, "Child" or "Spouse") so the care team knows who they are communicating with.

Device and notification data. If you enable push notifications, we collect a device push token and a device identifier so we can deliver notifications to your device and remove it when you sign out.

Website inquiries. When you request a demo on our website we collect your first and last name, phone number, email address, and company name and description.

The Gloria app does not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How We Use the Information

  • To operate the Services — delivering updates, photos, announcements, newsletters, calendars, and supply requests between care facilities and families.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send push notifications you have enabled, such as new updates or announcements.
  • To respond to demo requests and other inquiries.
  • To maintain internal records and improve our products and services.

Sharing Your Information

We will not sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties unless we have your permission or are required to do so by law. Content about a resident is shared only with the care facility's staff and the family members that facility has authorized. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the Services — including secure cloud hosting and storage, identity and sign-in services, and push notification delivery — and they process information only on our behalf and under contractual safeguards.

Health Information

Wellness updates shared by a care facility may include health-related information about a resident. Gloria processes this information solely to provide the Services to the facility and the families it authorizes. Where applicable law (such as HIPAA) requires it, Gloria's relationship with the care facility is governed by a written agreement covering the handling of protected health information. Questions about a resident's health information should be directed to the care facility first.

Security

We are committed to ensuring that your personal information is secure. Information travels between your device and our servers over encrypted connections, media is stored in access-controlled cloud storage with short-lived access links, sign-in is handled by a dedicated identity provider, and role-based permissions restrict every account to the residents and facility it has been authorized for. To prevent unauthorized access or disclosure, we have implemented suitable physical, electronic, and managerial procedures to safeguard the information we collect.

Data Retention

We retain account information and resident content for as long as the care facility maintains its subscription and your account remains active, and as needed to comply with legal obligations. When you sign out of the app, locally cached resident information is removed from your device. Facilities may request deletion of accounts or content at any time.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites. However, once you leave our site, we do not have control over external websites. We are not responsible for the protection and privacy of any information you provide while visiting such sites, and such sites are not governed by this privacy statement. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external websites you visit.

Controlling Your Personal Information

You may choose to restrict the collection or use of your personal information in the following ways:

  • You can disable push notifications at any time in the app's notification settings or your device settings.
  • Family and staff members can ask their care facility to update or remove their account or content shared about their loved one.
  • Contact dave@gloriatech.co if you wish to access, update, correct, or delete your personal information.
